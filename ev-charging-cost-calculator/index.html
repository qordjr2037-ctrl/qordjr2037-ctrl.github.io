What the result means

Use your bill rate for a personal estimate

The EIA default is a statewide residential average. Your utility, fixed fees, tier, time-of-use plan, taxes and season can change the actual price. Divide the variable electricity charge on your bill by billed kWh when you want a bill-based rate.

Charging loss matters: a battery receiving 45 kWh at 90% efficiency requires 50 kWh from the grid. This calculator applies that loss before multiplying by your electricity rate.
